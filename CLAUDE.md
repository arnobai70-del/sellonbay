# SellOnBay

Marketplace for ready-made, AI-built websites and small tools. Buyers pick a site, give a domain (or buy one here), and the seller makes it live in 1 to 7 days. Not Fiverr: sellers deliver the same product again and again. Optional paid customisation and "extra work" requests on top.

Full plan (Bengali): `docs/AI_Marketplace_Guide.pdf`.

## Business rules (keep consistent everywhere)

- Delivery: 1 to 7 days, the seller picks on the listing (owner, 2026-10-09; migration 0041). Express option: 24 hours for $10-20. A faster job can also be agreed with the buyer in the order chat.
- Buyer review: 48 hours, then auto-accept.
- Escrow: payment held until accepted. Seller payout weekly, 7 days after acceptance.
- Fee (owner, 2026-10-09): 30% on a sale when the seller price (with package and express) is under $20, 22% from $20 up; a flat 20% on custom, extra and trial work (`lib/config.ts`). Domains and hosting are extras.
- All numbers of this kind (days, hours, fees, limits, extras prices) live in `lib/config.ts` with a test; the name lives in `lib/brand.ts`. Do not write them anywhere else.
- Custom work and extra work: paid into escrow BEFORE work starts. Extra work = seller sends request (title, price, +days), buyer approves and funds it.
- Refunds: final after acceptance, except dispute cases (not as described, doesn't work, malware, copyright) and a 7-day bug-fix guarantee. Do not claim "no refunds ever" (not enforceable in EU/UK, chargebacks still happen).
- Seller protection: buyers review the LIVE site. The seller's real files (source code, digital products) are released as soon as the order is FUNDED (money in escrow), never before payment and never public, only through a signed link that expires in 24 hours (a new one can be requested, 5 an hour per order), with one licence key per order. Every download is written to the append-only `download_events` table (user, time, IP, file hash) as evidence for disputes. Protection for sellers: escrow, the 7-day hold before payout, disputes that need evidence (3 rejected disputes flag the account, `profiles.flagged`), and an optional short release delay for new or high-risk buyers (env `RELEASE_DELAY_MINUTES`, `RELEASE_DELAY_NEW_BUYER_DAYS`, `RELEASE_DELAY_HIGH_PRICE_CENTS`, all 0 = off). `products.code_url` stays column-revoked from clients and is only fetched by the server (`lib/delivery/*`, `app/api/orders/[id]/source`, `app/api/download/[token]`, SSRF-guarded). License is personal use on own domain, no resale or copying (see Terms). Migrations `0002`, `0012`.
- Chat blocks emails, phone numbers, outside-payment talk. Must be enforced server-side, the client filter is only a demo.
- Orders: the state machine is `lib/orders/machine.ts` (one table of legal moves, tested). Never set an order's state any other way: use `lib/orders/service.ts` (`move`, `fund`, `accept`, `deliver`, `cancel`). Money is recorded only through the ledger (`lib/ledger.ts`, keyed entries, never below zero). Payment providers are reached only through `lib/providers/payment`; their events only through `lib/orders/payments.ts`.
- Extra work: title up to 80 characters, $5 to $500, 1 to 3 days, only before delivery; the buyer pays it into escrow before the seller starts. Trial: at most $150, 3 to 5 days; its fee can be credited toward a full job with the same developer within 30 days (once).

## Digital products (`/apps/digital`) and the paid trial

Sixth shelf: Figma kits, No-code templates, Plugins, Scripts, AI automations (n8n, Make, Zapier workflows), Chatbots, AI prompts (ChatGPT, Midjourney, ComfyUI, SEO writing packs), Video templates (Premiere Pro, After Effects, CapCut) and, since 2026-10-20, Motion graphics, Stock video, Music, Sound effects, Graphics, Add-ons, Presentation, Print, Social media and Logo templates, Mockups, Photos, 3D models and Fonts (grouped into topics by `DIGITAL_TOPICS`; AI models and voice or avatar presets are deliberately NOT a category yet: pickle malware, licences, deepfake risk) (`DIGITAL_CATS`, `DIGITAL_TOOLS`, platform `digital`, delivery `download`, migration `0010`). They are products you use, not sites to put live, so checkout hides the domain, app-name and extras steps and shows "What you get". Platform rules are the SAME for every kind of product (see Business rules): escrow, 48 hours to check, seller paid 7 days after acceptance, files released when the order is funded. For digital products the buyer gets the files at once through the expiring link and a licence key, and checks them during the 48 hours; dispute reasons are not as described, does not work, harmful code, copyright. With "setup help" or "customisation" the seller works on it first. Demo orders mark as-is digital orders `instant` and serve a harmless dummy file.
Keeping it fair for both sides without feeling strict: sellers see a friendly "Listing strength" checklist (3+ pictures, a demo or view-only link, a clear description, 4+ included items); a demo link is REQUIRED (server-enforced, `NEEDS_DEMO` in `lib/apps.ts`) for Plugins, Scripts, AI automations and Chatbots because a buyer cannot judge them from pictures, optional for Figma kits and No-code templates. Buyers get a "Your protection" panel (escrow, review pack, fair disputes where the seller can fix first, real demos) and an Ask-the-seller link before buying. Also built: a free sample link (public https only, `rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow"`, `FEATURE_FREE_SAMPLE`, on by default), a limited trial copy (OFF by default, `FEATURE_TRIAL_COPY=1`; it is a SEPARATE cut-down file the seller uploads, private, one per buyer per product per week, one-hour signed link, logged; we never trim the real file) and a demo-quality ranking lift plus a "Demo included" badge (`lib/demoQuality.ts`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_FEATURE_DEMO_RANKING`). Rules in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
Safety: scripts and plugins are the riskiest listings. New digital listings go to `in_review`, sellers must tick that the files are clean and theirs, and a human reviews before approval. There is NO automatic malware scan yet (CLAUDE.md step 6 still open): do not approve a digital listing without opening the files in a sandbox. Starter digital products are made-up.
The paid trial is a fourth option on every developer profile (`trialOf` in `lib/developers.ts`): about a third of the Basic price rounded to $5, 3 days, same escrow and 48-hour review (migration `0011` allows pack `Trial`).

## Hosted demos (seller uploads)

Sellers upload a site zip on `/sell/new` (or give a live demo link). `POST /api/listings` checks the seller (role + authenticator), opens the zip with `lib/demoZip.ts` (allow-listed file types, size and entry limits, no path tricks, dotfiles dropped), then stores the files in the private `demos` bucket (`lib/demoStore.ts`). `/demo/<slug>/...` serves them from our domain but ALWAYS with `Content-Security-Policy: sandbox` and no `allow-same-origin`, so seller scripts cannot touch our cookies or session. Never add allow-same-origin and never put `/demo` behind the proxy. New listings are `in_review`: only the owner and admins can open them; strangers get 404 (product, preview and demo routes). Going live is an admin action (no admin UI yet: `update products set status = 'live' where slug = ...`). `/preview/<slug>` is the full-page preview (Desktop/Phone); listings without a hosted demo fall back to the generated `TemplatePage`. Product pages are `force-dynamic` because visibility depends on who asks; do not add `generateStaticParams` back, and never let a `catch` swallow Next's dynamic-render signals (see `isNextSignal`).

## Phone layout lessons

- A grid column written `1fr` grows to its widest child and pushes content off a real phone; write `minmax(0,1fr)` (the hero and app tiles broke this way). `overflow:hidden` on a section only hides the damage.
- Test phone width against the width you SET (360 or 390), never `innerWidth` or `visualViewport.width`: with mobile emulation both grow with the content, so an over-wide page looks fine. Compare `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` to the set width.

## Roles (owner, 2026-10-20)

Four roles: Visitor (not signed in: browses, opens previews and demos, cannot buy, hire, ask sellers or sell), Buyer (buys products, hires developers, asks sellers questions, own dashboard `/dashboard/buyer`), Seller (sells, own dashboard `/dashboard/seller`, cannot buy), Admin (runs the marketplace, `/dashboard/admin`). Only a buyer account buys: `mayBuy` in `lib/accounts.ts`, checked on the server in `/api/demo/orders`, `/api/hire`, `/api/presale` and the trial copy, and on the checkout and hire pages (`BuyersOnly` notice). Visitors have their own basic panel at `/guest` (`GuestPanel`: saved and recently viewed from this browser, what a visitor can do, the way to an account; signed-in people are sent to their dashboard; the person icon and the phone tab "You" lead there). Sign-up still picks Buyer or Seller (`app/(bare)/login/actions.ts`); demo mode (no Supabase) keeps the guest flow for the made-up examples.

## Site search (owner, 2026-10-21)

The top bar search (shelf picker "Everything", the default off shelf pages) shows results while typing (`GET /api/search`, module `catalog`, 90 a minute per address) and Enter opens `/search?q=` (noindex, filter by kind). One index in `lib/siteSearch.ts`: live and starter products, categories and topics, every page in `SEO_PAGES` (the full text of its built HTML, cut into sections at each h2, so a word deep in a page finds it), published blog posts and developers. Matching and ranking are pure in `lib/siteSearchCore.ts` (tested): every word must appear, part of a word counts, the title counts most; page and post links carry a `#:~:text=` fragment that scrolls to the words.

## Visitor analytics (owner, 2026-10-21)

Admin → Visitors (`/dashboard/admin/analytics`): visitors today and over 14 days (new and repeat), the most opened products, categories and pages for today, 7 or 30 days, product attraction (different people who opened a product and the share who clicked Buy, judged with `CONFIG.analytics.attraction`), and daily reports (`/dashboard/admin/analytics/report?day=`, with a CSV from `GET /api/admin/analytics/report`). `components/VisitTracker.tsx` in the `(site)` layout posts each page open and each click on a `/checkout?id=` link to `POST /api/track` (module `visitor`); it sends nothing when the browser has Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. No cookie: a visitor is a keyed hash of the address and browser (`ANALYTICS_SALT`, else `GUARD_SALT`), so one person on two devices counts twice; bots are skipped; one visitor counts once per day, event and key, repeats are capped at 50, and at most `perVisitorPerMinute` events a minute are kept. What counts is in `lib/analyticsCore.ts` (pure, tested); storage is `lib/analytics.ts` (migration 0052: `analytics_visitors`, `analytics_hits`, `analytics_reports`, server only; in memory without it). The cron job stores yesterday's report once, sends admins a `daily_report` notification and deletes rows older than `keepDays`. Days are UTC, like the Risk page. The privacy page says all this.

## Live chat (owner, 2026-10-21)

Tawk.to live chat shows on the public `(site)` pages only (`components/TawkChat.tsx`, loaded after the page settles; on phones the bubble sits above the tab bar). Ids come from the owner's embed code, overridable with `NEXT_PUBLIC_TAWK_PROPERTY_ID` / `NEXT_PUBLIC_TAWK_WIDGET_ID` ("off" turns it off). The CSP in `next.config.ts` allows `*.tawk.to`; the cookie notice and privacy page say the chat sets its own cookies and messages go to Tawk.to.

## Module maintenance (owner, 2026-10-20)

An admin can close one module (13 in `MODULES`, `lib/maintenance/config.ts`) while the rest of the site works. That file is the only place that says which page and API belongs to which module (a test checks every route); payment webhooks, cron jobs, `/api/health`, sign-in (`/login`), the audit log and the maintenance screen are never closed. Saved in `module_maintenance` (migration 0049, server only), history written by the database into the append-only `module_maintenance_history`; read by `lib/maintenance/state.ts` (10-second cache per instance, a change on this instance at once; a database error leaves modules OPEN). Three layers check it: `proxy.ts` (pages show `/maintenance/<module>` with 503, APIs answer 503 + `Retry-After`), every API handler starts with `maintenanceFor('<module>')` (a test checks each one), and `requireViewer` and the sign-up and reset actions check too. An admin with the authenticator code may still use a closed module to test it, except the admin module. Admin screen: Admin → System → Maintenance Manager (`/dashboard/admin/maintenance`: per module on/off, message, start and end, what is closed now, history; asks again before closing the admin dashboard or a fifth module); API `GET/POST /api/admin/maintenance`. Order timers keep running during maintenance. Blue-green deployment for a VPS is prepared in `deploy/` (`blue-green.sh deploy|rollback|status`, systemd unit, nginx example; tested with stand-ins, never run on a server); Hostinger's Node.js web hosting can only do the single-copy restart.

## Security lessons (do not undo)

- Tables need explicit grants (`supabase/migrations/0004_grants.sql`); RLS alone is not enough. Clients get the minimum columns, never TRUNCATE. New tables must add their grants in the same migration.
- Every protected page calls `requireViewer()` (`lib/supabase/viewer.ts`). `proxy.ts` is not enough: a Server Action redirect renders the next page without going through the proxy.
- Sellers and admins need aal2 (authenticator code) on every protected page, checked on the server.

## SEO (admin, October 2026)

`/dashboard/admin/seo` is the hub: blog posts, every page (`/seo/pages`), every live listing (`/seo/listings`), the automatic check (`/seo/check`), home words and Search Console codes. One list of public pages, `SEO_PAGES` in `lib/seo/pageList.ts` (digital topics included, as `/apps/digital?topic=<key>`), feeds the panel, page metadata (`pageMetadata`) and the sitemap; a new public page goes in that list with a description of 50 to 170 characters (tested). Listings get automatic words (`autoListingMeta` in `lib/seo/listingMeta.ts`: "Name · Category" and the seller's description, filled out when short) and a canonical address; an admin's words for `/product/<id>` are saved like a page's and win. Listings that are not live are kept out of search. The check (`lib/seo/check.ts`, pure and tested) flags missing, long, short and repeated titles and descriptions.
