Questions people ask

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You choose a site and give us your domain. The seller deploys it and shows you how to change text and images.

What if the site doesn't match the demo?

Don't accept it. Open a dispute and our team reviews the delivery log, messages and the live site. If it doesn't match the listing, you get a refund.

Can I get a refund after I accept?

Accepting makes the order final, so check carefully during your 48-hour review. Problems with security, copyright or a site that stops working are still covered by our bug-fix guarantee for 7 days.

Who owns the domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and you can move it any time.

How do sellers get paid?

Every week we pay out all earnings that have been accepted for at least 7 days. You choose a bank transfer or Payoneer.

Why can't we chat or pay outside Launchbay?

Payments made outside are not protected. Messages with emails, phone numbers or outside payment requests are blocked, and repeat attempts can end in a ban.
